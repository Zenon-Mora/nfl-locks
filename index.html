Weather, trends, travel: Weather rule: a dome, or wind under 10 mph with gusts under 20 and rain under 25%, is Over-friendly; sustained wind of 15+ mph, gusts of 30+, rain of 50%+, 20°F or colder, or a poor or wet field is Under-lean. When the forecast works against our O/U side, the totals tier drops one step (Favored → Lean → No strong edge), but the pick itself doesn't flip. Coach primetime records, venue records and head-to-head are tiebreakers only, and they count only when they come from the current coaches and quarterbacks. Older streaks (for example MIN 2-8 vs TB, NE 8-2 vs JAX, the Rams' 5 straight wins over DEN) never outweigh current injuries, lines and form, and we say so where they appear. Travel and rest (time zones crossed, body-clock kickoff time, days of rest, long-haul flights) is a small nudge that can change confidence or a close ATS call, never an SU pick on its own.
